Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Fencing Camera (the “App”) is an iOS application provided by ReelBreakdown—Aaron Li (“we,” “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

In short: Fencing Camera does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you record and enter stays on your device.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not have servers that receive your data.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Bout recordings (video files saved to your Photos Library)
  • Bout metadata you enter: fencer names, tournament and event names, weapon, scores, period information, and timestamps
  • App preferences and settings

None of this information leaves your device. We have no access to it.

2. Permissions the App Requests

The App asks for the following iOS permissions, used only for the features they enable:

  • Camera — to record bout video
  • Microphone — to record audio along with the video
  • Photo Library (add) — to save your recordings to your Photos Library
  • Photo Library (read) — to play back and display your recordings inside the App

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security or iOS Settings → Fencing Camera.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. No data is sent to any third party.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children, or from anyone else, because the App does not collect information at all. The App is suitable for general audiences and does not include advertising or external links to web content.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control retention and deletion:

  • Delete a bout from inside the App to remove its metadata
  • Delete the video from the Photos app to remove the recording
  • Uninstall the App to remove all App-managed data from your device

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
